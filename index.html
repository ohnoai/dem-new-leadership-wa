02 · The voters

Who actually chooses leadership?

Both Washington senators vote in their Senate Democratic caucus election. Washington's eight Democratic House members vote in their House caucus election. House office forms may require that you live in the member's district.

Don't know your district? Use the official House finder. A ZIP code isn't always enough to determine a district.

03 · Beyond the vote

The rest of the circus.

The state party and governor cannot cast congressional leadership votes. You can still ask them to address the issue publicly or communicate constituent concerns. Their official offices may limit responses to matters within their jurisdiction.

Important

Be precise about the vote.

The House Democratic caucus chooses its party leader; the full House separately elects the Speaker. Senate Democrats choose their own leader internally. State officials have no ballot in either election.
