      About, privacy & sources

      Independent civic advocacy. Not a government website or affiliated with a political party. House Democrats choose their caucus leader separately from the full House's election of a Speaker. Senate Democrats choose their own leader. State and party officials have influence, not a congressional caucus ballot.

      Your details, drafts and progress stay in this browser. No account, analytics or backend. Nothing is sent from this site: you send each message yourself on the official's own contact form. Browser data can be cleared or unavailable in private mode, and does not sync across devices.

      District matching uses the U.S. Census Bureau's 2020 ZIP Code Tabulation Area to 119th Congressional District relationship file. Census ZIP areas approximate postal ZIP codes, so a match is "most likely", not certain. ZIP codes that cross a district line show every possible district, and the state's district finder checks your exact address.
