About the campaign, privacy & sources

Independent civic advocacy. Not a government website or affiliated with a political party. House Democrats choose their caucus leader separately from the full House's election of a Speaker. Senate Democrats choose their own leader. State and party officials have influence, not a congressional caucus ballot.

Drafts, optional personal details and self-reported actions stay in this browser. No account, analytics, or backend. Browser data can be cleared or unavailable in private mode. No cross-device sync.

Optional details personalize untouched template fields. Your edits, including manual signatures, take precedence. Full and concise letters each keep their own edits. District lookup uses official boundaries, not a ZIP-code guess; your address is entered on the official finder, not here.
